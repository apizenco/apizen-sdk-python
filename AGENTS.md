# apizen-sdk-python

## Commits and comments

- Code comments, docs and commit messages are always in English.
- Commits follow [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org): `type(scope): subject`, with `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `style`, `refactor`, `perf`, `test`, `build`, `ci`, `chore` or `revert`.
- Keep the subject short (about 60 characters at most), in the imperative, without a final period. One change per commit: small, focused commits.
- No AI attribution in commits or pull requests (no `Co-Authored-By` or "Generated with" lines).
